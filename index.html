01 / Overview

Adapt the whole arm when the space changes.

Reaching the target with the end effector alone does not guarantee a feasible operation. EgoAdapt studies whether human whole-arm adaptation experience can help a robot handle spatial constraints absent from its robot adaptation demonstrations.

We vary spatial constraints while preserving task semantics, then transfer the adaptation signal into a shared latent action space.
